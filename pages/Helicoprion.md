- *spiral saw*
- a genus of shark-like [cartilaginous fish](Chondrichthyes) in the clade [[Holocephali]] and the order [[Eugeneodontiformes]], which lived in the early to middle [[Permian]], characterized by distinctive tooth structures called 'tooth whorls'
- first named in [[1899]]
